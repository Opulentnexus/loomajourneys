MEDICAL TOURISM

Treatment.
Travel. Accommodation. Coordination.

Looma can coordinate the travel side of a medical journey — helping organize transportation, accommodation and destination logistics around the traveller's treatment schedule.

Looma provides travel and coordination assistance. Medical diagnosis, treatment decisions, clinical advice and treatment outcomes remain the responsibility of the treating healthcare professionals and hospital.
Hospital CoordinationTravel-side coordination around scheduled hospital visits.
Airport TransfersArrival and departure transportation planning.
AccommodationStay options for patients and accompanying attendants.
Attendant SupportTravel coordination for family members or companions.
Local TransportationPlanned movement between accommodation and required destinations.
Interpreter AssistanceCoordination support where language assistance is required and available.
Post-Treatment StayAccommodation and travel planning after treatment.
Travel CoordinationA single point for the non-clinical travel arrangements.
