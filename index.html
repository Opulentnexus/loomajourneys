Treatment.

Looma can coordinate the travel side of a medical journey — helping organize transportation, accommodation and destination logistics around the traveller's treatment schedule.

Looma provides travel and coordination assistance. Medical diagnosis, treatment decisions, clinical advice and treatment outcomes remain the responsibility of the treating healthcare professionals and hospital.
